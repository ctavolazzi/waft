## Background

### Origin
Aziah Calderon is a quantum scientist of extraordinary brilliance, working at the forefront of teleportation technology at Teleport Massive. Her life was changed forever by the loss of someone she loved - a loss she refuses to accept as permanent.

### Key Events
1. [[Events/Joining_Teleport_Massive]] - Recruited by Fai Wei for her exceptional abilities

## Psychology

### Core Motivation
> To bring back the one she lost, no matter the cost to physics, reality, or herself.

### Fears
- That death truly is final
- That she will forget what they were like
- That she will succeed and it won't be the same

### Desires
- Reunion with the lost one
- To prove the universe can be bent
- Validation that love is stronger than physics

### Fatal Flaw
Grief-driven tunnel vision. She cannot see what her quest might cost others, or what it might cost reality itself.

## Story Function

### Narrative Purpose
Aziah is the vehicle through which we explore the question: *What would you sacrifice to undo death?* Her journey reveals the nature of Teleport Massive, the structure of reality in this universe, and ultimately challenges our understanding of what it means to let go.

## Meta Notes

> *DM/Author notes not visible to the "reader"*

Aziah is our lens into the Teleport Massive universe. Through her eyes, we discover:
- How teleportation actually works
- What's really happening at TM
- The nature of Scinting
- The truth about SWAB/SWAE

She begins sympathetic (who wouldn't want to bring back a loved one?) but her journey should force readers to question whether what she's doing is right.

The Pratchett influence: She should be intelligent enough to see the absurdity of her situation, even as she pursues it. Moments of dark humor amid genuine pathos.
