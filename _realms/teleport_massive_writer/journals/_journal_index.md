# Journal Index

> **Internal Thoughts & External Actions**
> **The Dual-Track Character Record System**

---

## Purpose

Every character maintains two parallel records:

### Internal Journals
> *What they actually think, feel, and know*

Located in: `journals/internal/`

These capture:
- True motivations
- Hidden knowledge
- Secret feelings about others
- Internal conflicts
- Plans and intentions

### External Records
> *What others could observe*

Located in: `journals/external/`

These capture:
- Actions taken
- Dialogue spoken
- Body language
- What others might infer
- Discrepancies between appearance and reality

---

## Why Two Tracks?

This system enables:

1. **Dramatic Irony**: Reader knows what characters don't
2. **Unreliable Narration**: Characters can lie or self-deceive
3. **Complex Motivation**: Actions can contradict thoughts
4. **Who Knows What**: Track information flow precisely
5. **Character Depth**: Internal life separate from presentation

## Journal Entry Workflow

### After Each Scene

1. **Identify POV character**: Their internal journal gets detailed entry
2. **Identify witnesses**: What could they observe?
3. **Update internal journal**: True thoughts/feelings
4. **Update external record**: Observable actions
5. **Note discrepancies**: Where do inside/outside differ?
