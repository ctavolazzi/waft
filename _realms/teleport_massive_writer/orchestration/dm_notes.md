## Tone & Style Reminders

### The Pratchett Touch
- Footnotes are commentary, not just information
- The narrator is aware they're telling a story
- Truth wrapped in absurdity
- Darkness acknowledged but not wallowed in
- Characters are people, not archetypes
- Every joke earns its place

### Sentence-Level
- Vary rhythm
- Short punchy sentences after long ones
- End chapters on hooks or revelations
- Dialogue should reveal character
- Actions > described emotions

### Meta-Narrative Elements
- The manuscript IS real in-universe
- Reader is addressed (carefully)
- Fourth wall is a door, not a wall

## Emergency Protocols

### If Stuck on a Scene
1. Skip to next scene, come back
2. Write the dialogue only, fill in later
3. Write what happens, not how (placeholder prose)
4. Ask: what does POV character want RIGHT NOW?

### If Story Has Gone Off Rails
1. Find last scene that worked
2. Identify the wrong turn
3. Don't be afraid to cut
4. Remember: editing is rewriting

### If a Character Isn't Working
1. What do they want?
2. What do they fear?
3. What would they never do?
4. If they're boring, give them a secret
