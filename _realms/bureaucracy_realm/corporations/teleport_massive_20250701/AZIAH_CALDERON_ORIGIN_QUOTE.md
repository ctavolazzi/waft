**Date**: 2026-01-20  
**Character**: Aziah Calderon

## The Quote

> "They said death was final. They said the distance between us was absolute. They must be wrong.
>
> I will come back to her, even if I have to rewrite Time Itself."
>
> — **Aziah Calderon**

---

## Context

Aziah Calderon is the man responsible for:
- The Scint
- Teleportation Technology
- Everything else in the Teleport Massive universe

His motivation: To be reunited with his lost love, his wife who was taken by cancer.

This quote represents the emotional core that drives all of Teleport Massive's technology and research. It is the origin story of everything.
