🎮 Git Gud

9 unique cards • Print 12 copies for full 108-card deck • Click cards to flip

Introduce bug

Introduce a bug in another players repo. The other player must add 1 to their bugs.

The next Player can add the same card to add more bugs to the following Player

Merge

You hand over one of your cards of choice to another player of choice.

Fix bug

Guess a number from 1 to 6 roll a die, if you hit the number remove 2 bugs.
If your guess is off by one, remove 1 bug

Rebase

You get a card at random of a player of choice.

Squash commits

Remove one card of choice.

Take one additional card.

Commit

Guess even or uneven, roll a die, if your guess is correct, remove 1 bug, else add 2 bugs

This card also can be used to avoid "Introduce bug" if laid immediately without hesitation. This however invalidates the primary intention of this card.

Retire repo

You retire your repo. The next Player adds 1 to their bugs.

This card is not stackable.

Pair programming

Together with a player of choice each roll a die. If the eye-count differs by more than 1 add 1 bug else remove 2 bugs

Clone repo

You start from a fresh clone.

Roll a die and the eye-count is the number of cards you can replace.
