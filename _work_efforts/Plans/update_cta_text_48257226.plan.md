#Update CTA Text to Be More Exciting and Enticing

## Overview

Remove the "respond within the hour" time promise from both CTA locations and replace with more exciting, value-focused copy that encourages action.

## Files to Update

### 1. [src/index.html](src/index.html)

- **Line 151** (Hero section): Replace `"Click to email your weird question. I'll respond within the hour."` with more exciting copy
- **Line 357** (Final CTA section): Replace `"Send me your weird question. I'll get back to you within the hour."` with more exciting copy

## Proposed Copy Options

### Option 1: Value-focused

- Hero: `"Click to email your weirdest question. Let's solve it together."`
- Final CTA: `"Send me your weirdest question. Let's crack it together."`

### Option 2: Action-oriented

- Hero: `"Click to email your weird question. Ready to solve the unsolvable?"`
- Final CTA: `"Send me your weird question. Your problem deserves a real answer."`

### Option 3: Curiosity-driven

- Hero: `"Click to email your weirdest question. What's keeping you up?"`
- Final CTA: `"Send me your weird question. Let's figure it out."`
