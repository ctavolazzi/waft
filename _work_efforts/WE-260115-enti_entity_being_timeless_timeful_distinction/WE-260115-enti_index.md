## Objective
Refine the conceptual distinction between Entities (Pantheon) and Beings to reflect their fundamental nature:

- **Entities**: Timeless, stable, hold Aspects of Creation that shouldn't change until evidence proves otherwise. Forces that bind reality together. Minimal movement/change.
- **Beings**: Timeful, dynamic, change rapidly, move a lot, learn and evolve.

This distinction should be reflected in documentation, code attributes, and system design.

## Core Philosophy

### Entities (Pantheon) - Timeless
- **Nature**: Forces that Bind Reality Together
- **Stability**: Hold Aspects of Creation that should not change until body of evidence collected by beings proves change is needed
- **Movement**: Minimal - they don't move much
- **Change Rate**: Very slow, only when evidence demands it
- **Purpose**: Maintain stability, preserve fundamental aspects of creation
- **Examples**: Magistrate (Precedent), Judge (Judgment), Fae (Creativity), Storyteller (Narrative)

### Beings - Timeful
- **Nature**: Dynamic agents in realities
- **Stability**: Constantly changing, learning, evolving
- **Movement**: High - they move a lot and change things rapidly
- **Change Rate**: Rapid - skills, memories, states change frequently
- **Purpose**: Explore, learn, evolve, collect evidence
- **Examples**: All Being instances spawned into realities
