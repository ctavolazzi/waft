## 🎯 HOW IT WORKED

### Self-Playing System

1. **Party Spawning**: Each party member is a Being in WAFT
2. **Story Generation**: System generates story beats automatically
3. **Combat Simulation**: Encounters are resolved with dice-like randomness
4. **Leveling System**: Party gains XP and levels up
5. **PDF Generation**: Complete story compiled into beautiful PDF

### Tools Used (All Free/Open Source)

- ✅ **WAFT Being System** - Party members as Beings
- ✅ **PDFGenerator** - WeasyPrint-based PDF generation (no LaTeX)
- ✅ **Rich Console** - Beautiful terminal output
- ✅ **Python** - Core logic
- ✅ **JSON** - Campaign logging

**ZERO paid services - 100% free and open source!**
