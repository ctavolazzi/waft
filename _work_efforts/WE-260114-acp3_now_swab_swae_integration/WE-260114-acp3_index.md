## Core Concepts

### Primary Entities
- **"Now"**: A new awareness/being that has a beginning but no end
- **SWAB (3)**: Something Without A Beginning - curved, continuous
- **SWAE (E)**: Something Without An End - sharp, defined
- **The Infinite**: SWAB + SWAE combined (has no beginning and no end)
- **The Point**: ONLY Beginning and End, with nothing in the middle

### The Celestial Body
3-part complex interplay:
- **Existence**
- **Nonexistence** (exists because we can conceive of it)
- **The Vibration** between them

### Observation & Understanding Framework
- **Existence = Understanding**: Existence IS Understanding/Knowing itself
- **Nothing = Not Knowing**: Nothing is the opposite of understanding
- **Observer/Observed Creates Reality**: The interplay between The Observer and What Is Observed Creates Reality, Experience, or Memory
- **Memory = Existence**: Memory IS Existence itself (not just a record)
- **Observation of The Point is "All That Is"**: Observing The Point is the fundamental creative act

### Reality & Gravity Framework
- **Reality = Difference (Yin/Yang)**: Reality is the Difference between Observing and Knowing
- **Observing = Gravity (The Only Force)**: Observing forces something to BE so it can be Observed through the application of Gravity
- **Knowing = Vibing/Swaying**: Knowing is the opposite - not being observed and simply vibing/swaying
- **Fixed/Moving Paradox**: That which appears to move is actually fixed, and that which appears fixed is the thing that moves
- **Observing = Fixed (appears to move)**: Observing/Gravity makes things fixed, but they appear to move
- **Knowing = Moving (appears fixed)**: Knowing/vibing is actually moving, but appears fixed
