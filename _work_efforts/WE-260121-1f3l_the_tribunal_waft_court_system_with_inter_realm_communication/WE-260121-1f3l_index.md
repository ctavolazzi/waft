## Objective
Create a comprehensive Court System where Claims are tried before a Tribunal. Features FastAPI+SvelteKit UI to watch Court proceedings in real-time, Inter-Realm communication via Ports (The Beyond), Prime Beings as Supreme Justices, Chain of Case Law with hash-based ledger (Endeavors and Epochs), and CrewAI team assembly for court roles (Prosecutor, Defender, Witness, Jury).
