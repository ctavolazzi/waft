## What We Built Together

### The Karma Depletion Analysis

We discovered that when beings run out of karma, the system currently raises an `InsufficientKarmaError` and beings get stuck. We explored five potential mechanisms and recommended the Basic Lifetime Grant—a solution that maintains the economic loop while preserving the value of karma.

**Key Insight**: Zero karma doesn't mean death. It means a reset to basics with the opportunity to earn your way back.

This analysis wasn't just technical—it touched on the philosophical question of what happens when resources run out, and how systems can be designed to maintain continuity while preserving meaning.

### The Comprehensive Audit

We examined the entire project state and found it healthy overall. We identified that the karma system, while functional, has some implementation gaps. More importantly, we documented everything clearly so future work can proceed with full awareness.

The audit revealed not just technical details, but the care and thoughtfulness that goes into building WAFT. The systematic approach, comprehensive documentation, and clean codebase all speak to quality work.
