# WAFT Sierra-Style Adventure Game - Context Document

> **Purpose**: Provide Claude Code with rich lore, characters, and concepts for building a Sierra-style adventure game with EGA graphics and text parser.

---

## 🎮 GAME PREMISE

**Title Suggestion**: "TELEPORT MASSIVE: Scint Traversal" or "The Reality Fracture"

**Setting**: Year 2111, Post-Singularity Earth. Humanity has joined the Galactic Federation. Teleport Massive, the corporation that invented real-life teleportation, is one of the most powerful entities in reality.

**Tagline**: *"They said death was final. They must be wrong."*

**Core Conflict**: A quantum scientist's quest to resurrect her lost love collides with a detective investigating corporate conspiracy—both discovering that reality itself is at stake.

## 👤 MAIN CHARACTERS

### Dr. Aziah Calderon (Playable Protagonist Option 1)
**Role**: Quantum Scientist at Teleport Massive  
**Quote**: *"They said death was final. They must be wrong."*

**Background**:
- Brilliant scientist working at Teleport Massive
- Lost someone she loved during a teleportation accident
- The loss was not death, but being "scattered across quantum states, existing everywhere and nowhere simultaneously"
- Refuses to accept this as permanent

**Personality**:
- Works obsessively, often through the night
- Detached from social interaction
- Moments of startling clarity amid general distraction
- Intelligent enough to see the absurdity of her situation, even as she pursues it

**Motivations**:
- Reunion with the lost one
- To prove the universe can be bent
- Validation that love is stronger than physics

**Fears**:
- That death truly is final
- That she will forget what they were like
- That she will succeed and it won't be the same

**Fatal Flaw**: Grief-driven tunnel vision—cannot see what her quest might cost others, or reality itself.

**Key Quote for Dialogue**:
> "Every equation is a prayer. Every experiment is a desperate grasp at hope."

## 🔮 KEY CONCEPTS FOR PUZZLES

### Scints (Reality Fractures)
**Definition**: Points where reality fractures and diverges, creating alternate timelines.

**Types**:
- **SYNTAX_TEAR**: Formatting/structural errors
- **LOGIC_FRACTURE**: Contradictions, paradoxes
- **SAFETY_VOID**: Dangerous content/leaks
- **HALLUCINATION**: Fabricated information

**Game Mechanic**: Player might need to identify and stabilize scints to progress.

---

### Phaseburn
**Definition**: A perilous side effect of dimensional travel that fractures one's existence.

**Symptoms**:
- Physical scarring
- Psychological trauma
- Fractured existence across dimensions
- Sometimes: glimpses of other realities

**Game Use**: Phaseburners could be NPCs with cryptic clues about reality.

---

### SWAB & SWAE (Cosmic Artifacts)
**SWAB** (Something Without A Beginning):
- Symbol: 3 (curved shape)
- No beginning—always was
- Represents continuity and flow

**SWAE** (Something Without An End):
- Symbol: E (sharp shape)
- No end—always will be
- Represents definition and edge

**The Infinite**: SWAB + SWAE combined (has no beginning and no end)

**Game Use**: MacGuffin items that could unlock dimensional travel or resurrection.

---

### The Vibration
**Definition**: The oscillation between existence and nonexistence—the rhythm that determines what is real.

## 🎯 SUGGESTED PUZZLE TYPES

### Inventory Puzzles
- Combine SWAB artifact with SWAE artifact
- Use quantum calibration device on unstable portal
- Present ID badge to security terminal

### Text Parser Commands
- `EXAMINE TERMINAL` → Reveals login prompt
- `USE BADGE ON SCANNER` → Opens secure door
- `TALK TO PHASEBURNER` → Cryptic clues
- `CALIBRATE PORTAL WITH DEVICE` → Stabilize reality fracture
- `READ MANIFEST` → Corporate secrets

### Logic Puzzles
- Decode Teleport Massive security codes
- Stabilize a SCINT by fixing logical contradictions
- Navigate The Between by following the correct "vibration"

### Dialogue Trees
- Convince security guard you have clearance
- Extract information from traumatized Phaseburner
- Negotiate with Underground tech dealer

## 📖 SUGGESTED GAME STRUCTURE (10-15 min)

### Act 1: The Hook (3-4 minutes)
- Player chooses Aziah or Sam perspective
- **Aziah path**: Starts in lab, discovers critical breakthrough, needs specific component
- **Sam path**: Starts at crime scene, finds clue pointing to TM

### Act 2: Investigation (5-6 minutes)
- Navigate TM Headquarters or Underground
- Gather key items, talk to NPCs
- Encounter a reality fracture (SCINT puzzle)
- Learn about Phaseburn and The Between

### Act 3: Climax (3-4 minutes)
- Enter The Between or TM's secret lab
- Final puzzle combining artifacts/knowledge
- Confrontation with consequences of choice
- Multiple endings based on decisions
