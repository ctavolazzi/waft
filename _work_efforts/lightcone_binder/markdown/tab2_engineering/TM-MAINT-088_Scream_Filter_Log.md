# TM-MAINT-088: Maintenance Log - "The Scream Filter"

**Document Type**: Maintenance Checklist / Log  
**Classification**: INTERNAL USE ONLY  
**Tab**: 2 - Engineering & Hardware

## Content Overview

Maintenance procedures and log entries for the acoustic dampening system ("Scream Filter") that prevents "soul residue" audio emissions from Fulgurite Cores from affecting personnel.

## Maintenance Procedures

### ROUTINE INSPECTION (Weekly)

**Checklist**:
- [ ] Visual inspection of filter layers (check for discoloration)
- [ ] Acoustic level test (should be < 20dB with Core active)
- [ ] Filter integrity check (no tears or gaps in shielding)
- [ ] Replacement date verification (Mark-V filters: 6-month lifespan)

**Tools Required**:
- Phase-Blind acoustic meter
- Visual inspection camera (Phase-Blind filtered)
- Filter replacement kit (Mark-V)

### FILTER REPLACEMENT PROCEDURE

**When to Replace**:
- Acoustic levels exceed 20dB
- Visual discoloration (filter "saturated" with residue)
- Scheduled replacement (6 months)
- Emergency replacement (filter failure)

**Steps**:
1. **Shutdown Core** (if possible) or activate emergency acoustic isolation
2. **Don Phase-Blind Visors** (all personnel)
3. **Remove old filter** (Mark-IV or previous)
   - Note: Old filters may "weep" - contain residue in disposal bags
4. **Install new filter** (Mark-V)
   - Ensure proper seal (no gaps)
   - Verify acoustic rating (should be < 20dB)
5. **Dispose of old filter** (incinerate in lead-lined furnace)
6. **Test system** (verify acoustic levels acceptable)
7. **Document replacement** (log entry with date, technician, reason)

### EMERGENCY REPLACEMENT

**Trigger**: Filter failure during active Core operation

**Symptoms**:
- Acoustic levels spike (> 40dB)
- Personnel report "hearing voices" or "crying sounds"
- Filter shows visible damage or saturation
- Core stability compromised

**Procedure**:
1. **Immediate isolation** (activate emergency acoustic barriers)
2. **Evacuate non-essential personnel** (those without Phase-Blind Visors)
3. **Emergency replacement** (follow standard procedure, accelerated)
4. **Post-replacement check** (verify Core stability, personnel status)

## Technical Specifications

**Filter Type**: Mark-V Acoustic Dampening Shielding
- **Material**: Multi-layer lead/ceramic composite
- **Thickness**: 15cm (6 layers)
- **Acoustic Rating**: < 20dB attenuation
- **Lifespan**: 6 months (normal operation)
- **Replacement Time**: 45 minutes (routine), 15 minutes (emergency)

**Previous Versions**:
- Mark-IV: 4-month lifespan, 25dB rating (replaced due to frequent failures)
- Mark-III: 3-month lifespan, 30dB rating (inadequate)
- Mark-II: 2-month lifespan, 35dB rating (unacceptable)

## Safety Warnings

**CRITICAL**: Do not perform maintenance without Phase-Blind Visors
- Acoustic emissions can induce Phase Burn
- "Soul residue" is cognitohazardous
- Filter disposal requires special handling (incineration only)

**If filter fails during operation**:
- Immediate isolation required
- Evacuate non-essential personnel
- Emergency replacement protocol
- Medical check for all exposed personnel
