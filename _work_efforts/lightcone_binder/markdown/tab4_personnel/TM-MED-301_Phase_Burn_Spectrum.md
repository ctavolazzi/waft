# TM-MED-301: Chart - The Phase Burn Spectrum

**Document Type**: Medical Progression Chart  
**Classification**: INTERNAL USE ONLY  
**Tab**: 4 - Personnel & Medical

## Content Overview

Medical chart documenting the progression of "Phase Burn" - the psychological and neurological damage caused by exposure to teleportation technology, Light Cone flicker events, or memetic contamination. This chart tracks the four stages of temporal dissociation that occur when human consciousness interacts with unstable reality fields.

## Key Sections

### CHART OVERVIEW

**Purpose**: Diagnostic tool for identifying Phase Burn severity
**Use**: Field assessment, medical screening, post-exposure evaluation
**Classification**: INTERNAL USE ONLY (medical data, not classified)

### THE PHASE BURN SPECTRUM

**Progression Model**: Linear progression through four stages
**Irreversibility**: Stages 1-2 may be treatable, Stages 3-4 are permanent
**Onset Time**: Varies by exposure type and duration

---

### STAGE 1: DÉJÀ VU (Mild)

**Severity**: Mild  
**Color Code**: Green  
**Reversibility**: Reversible with treatment

**Symptoms**:
- Frequent déjà vu episodes (3-5 per day)
- Mild temporal disorientation
- Brief "time slips" (seconds of confusion)
- Headaches after exposure
- Difficulty remembering recent events

**Neurological Indicators**:
- EEG shows minor temporal lobe anomalies
- Neural coherence: 0.85-0.90 (slightly below normal 0.95)
- No structural brain damage

**Treatment**:
- Remove from exposure source
- Class-B Amnestics (mild memory suppression)
- Temporal recalibration therapy
- Recovery time: 1-2 weeks

**Prognosis**: Excellent (95% full recovery)

---

### STAGE 2: TEMPORAL ECHOES (Moderate)

**Severity**: Moderate  
**Color Code**: Yellow  
**Reversibility**: Partially reversible

**Symptoms**:
- "Echo" memories (remembering events that haven't happened)
- Temporal confusion (uncertainty about when events occurred)
- Difficulty distinguishing past from present
- "Time loops" (feeling stuck in repeating moments)
- Increased anxiety and disorientation

**Neurological Indicators**:
- EEG shows significant temporal lobe disruption
- Neural coherence: 0.70-0.85 (moderate degradation)
- Minor structural changes in hippocampus

**Treatment**:
- Immediate removal from exposure
- Class-A Amnestics (stronger memory suppression)
- Intensive temporal recalibration
- Neural coherence restoration therapy
- Recovery time: 1-3 months

**Prognosis**: Good (70% significant improvement, 30% permanent Stage 2)

---

### STAGE 3: REALITY FLICKER (Severe)

**Severity**: Severe  
**Color Code**: Orange  
**Reversibility**: Irreversible

**Symptoms**:
- Reality "flickers" (brief moments where reality seems to shift)
- Loss of temporal linearity (time feels non-sequential)
- Difficulty distinguishing real from unreal
- "Ghost memories" (vivid memories of events that never happened)
- Paranoia and existential dread
- Subject may claim to "see the future" or "remember alternate timelines"

**Neurological Indicators**:
- EEG shows severe temporal lobe dysfunction
- Neural coherence: 0.50-0.70 (severe degradation)
- Significant structural damage to hippocampus and temporal cortex
- Neural pathways showing "reality bleed" (signals from alternate timelines)

**Treatment**:
- Permanent removal from field work
- Class-A Amnestics (ongoing, to prevent further degradation)
- Reality stabilization therapy (teach subject to ignore flickers)
- Neural coherence maintenance (prevent progression to Stage 4)
- No recovery possible, only management

**Prognosis**: Poor (permanent disability, managed decline)

---

### STAGE 4: FULL TEMPORAL DISSOCIATION (Critical)

**Severity**: Critical  
**Color Code**: Red  
**Reversibility**: Irreversible, terminal

**Symptoms**:
- Complete loss of temporal linearity
- Subject claims to "remember the future"
- Subject attempts to walk through solid walls (believes they're in different timeline)
- Subject no longer recognizes linear time
- "Reality blindness" (cannot distinguish between timelines)
- Complete loss of identity continuity
- Subject may claim to be "multiple people" or "from multiple timelines"

**Neurological Indicators**:
- EEG shows catastrophic temporal lobe failure
- Neural coherence: < 0.50 (critical degradation)
- Severe structural damage throughout temporal cortex
- Neural pathways completely disrupted
- "Reality bleed" overwhelming normal neural signals

**Treatment**:
- Institutionalization required
- Continuous Class-A Amnestic administration
- Reality containment protocols
- End-of-life care (Stage 4 is terminal)
- Average survival: 6-18 months after Stage 4 onset

**Prognosis**: Terminal (100% mortality, no recovery possible)

### EXPOSURE SOURCES

**High Risk**:
- Direct Light Cone exposure (teleportation events)
- Light Cone flicker events (reality instability)
- Fulgurite Core operations (consciousness extraction)
- Memetic contamination (Sector 7, high saturation zones)

**Medium Risk**:
- Prolonged proximity to teleportation hubs
- Scream Filter failures (acoustic exposure)
- Greys observation (if entity stabilizes)
- Secondary exposure (contact with Stage 3+ subjects)

**Low Risk**:
- Brief proximity to hubs
- Administrative work (no field exposure)
- Proper Phase-Blind Visor usage
