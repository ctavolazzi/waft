## Visual Description

### Main Diagram
- **Human Stick Figure**: Gender-neutral, simplified outline, centered on page
- **Cone of Reality**: Projection extending outward/upward from figure
  - Cone originates from figure's center (chest/heart area)
  - Expands outward in a cone shape
  - Represents the "reality field" projected by human consciousness

### Labels
- **"Event Horizon"**: At the outer boundary of the cone (where reality becomes uncertain)
- **"Chaos Gradient"**: Within the cone, showing increasing disorder toward edges
- **"The Dark (Xenos Habitat)"**: Outside the cone, where aliens hide

### Hand-drawn Elements
- **Red Circles**: Around "blind spots" where aliens hide
  - 3-5 circles scattered outside the cone
  - Hand-drawn appearance (not perfect circles)
  - Red color (if color available, otherwise marked with "RED" label)

### Technical Annotations
- Arrow pointing from figure to cone: "Reality Projection Vector"
- Dotted lines showing "observation boundaries"
- Small text: "Do not observe directly - induces temporal feedback"
