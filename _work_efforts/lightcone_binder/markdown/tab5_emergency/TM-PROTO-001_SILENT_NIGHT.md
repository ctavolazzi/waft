# TM-PROTO-001: Protocol SILENT NIGHT

**Document Type**: Emergency Protocol  
**Classification**: TOP SECRET // ORACLE EYES ONLY  
**Tab**: 5 - Emergency Protocols

## Content Overview

Emergency protocol for Light Cone flicker events - reality instability incidents where the teleportation field becomes unstable and reality begins to "flicker" between timelines. Protocol SILENT NIGHT is activated when reality flicker is detected, requiring immediate containment and amnestic deployment to prevent timeline contamination.

**Purpose**: Contain and neutralize Light Cone flicker events
**Threat Level**: CRITICAL (reality instability)

### PHASE 1: DETECTION & ALERT

**Step 1.1: Detection**
- Monitor Light Cone stability (continuous)
- Watch for flicker events (reality instability indicators)
- Check Subject K predictions (early warning system)
- Verify timeline bleed (alternate reality signals)

**Step 1.2: Alert**
- Activate Protocol SILENT NIGHT (immediate)
- Alert all personnel (evacuation signal)
- Notify Site Director and Chief Ontological Officer
- Contact Security Commander (Ext. 000)

**Step 1.3: Assessment**
- Determine flicker severity (mild/moderate/severe/critical)
- Identify affected area (flicker radius)
- Assess personnel exposure (who was in affected zone)
- Estimate contamination risk (timeline bleed extent)

**Response Time**: < 60 seconds from detection

---

### PHASE 2: CONTAINMENT

**Step 2.1: Evacuation**
- Evacuate all personnel from affected area (immediate)
- Use Phase-Blind evacuation routes (prevent observation)
- Account for all personnel (headcount at safe zones)
- Isolate affected area (seal all access points)

**Step 2.2: Isolation**
- Activate containment barriers (reality stabilization fields)
- Deploy Phase-Blind shields (prevent timeline observation)
- Seal affected area (no entry/exit)
- Monitor containment integrity (continuous)

**Step 2.3: Stabilization**
- Attempt Light Cone stabilization (if possible)
- Deploy reality anchors (temporal stabilization devices)
- Monitor flicker frequency (track stability)
- Prepare for amnestic deployment (if stabilization fails)

**Response Time**: < 5 minutes from activation

### PHASE 3: AMNESTIC DEPLOYMENT

**Step 3.1: Preparation**
- Prepare Class-A Amnestics (strong memory suppression)
- Load aerosol deployment systems
- Verify personnel are in safe zones (no exposure)
- Confirm containment is secure (no leaks)

**Step 3.2: Deployment**
- Deploy amnestic aerosols (affected area only)
- Monitor deployment effectiveness (memory suppression verification)
- Ensure no personnel exposure (safe zone confirmation)
- Document deployment (time, location, dosage)

**Step 3.3: Verification**
- Verify memory suppression (personnel screening)
- Check for timeline contamination (reality bleed detection)
- Assess Phase Burn symptoms (medical evaluation)
- Confirm containment success (flicker cessation)

**Response Time**: < 15 minutes from activation

### WARNINGS & CRITICAL NOTES

**⚠️ CRITICAL WARNINGS**:

1. **DO NOT OBSERVE FLICKER EVENTS DIRECTLY**
   - Observation can stabilize alternate timelines
   - Use Phase-Blind monitoring equipment only
   - Direct observation = timeline contamination

2. **DO NOT ENTER AFFECTED AREA**
   - Reality is unstable (may shift between timelines)
   - Personnel may become trapped in alternate timelines
   - Entry = extreme Phase Burn risk

3. **DO NOT ATTEMPT TO "FIX" FLICKER**
   - Flicker events are reality-level phenomena
   - Attempting to fix may worsen instability
   - Containment and amnestics only

4. **DO NOT DELAY AMMNESTIC DEPLOYMENT**
   - Timeline contamination spreads rapidly
   - Delayed deployment = increased contamination
   - Immediate deployment required

### ESCALATION PROTOCOL

**If Containment Fails**:
- Activate Protocol JUDGMENT DAY (tactical nuclear sterilization)
- Prepare for site-wide destruction
- Evacuate all personnel (immediate)
- Deploy final containment measures

**If Timeline Contamination Spreads**:
- Expand containment zone (larger area)
- Increase amnestic deployment (higher dosage)
- Consider site-wide evacuation (if contamination severe)
- Prepare for Protocol JUDGMENT DAY (if spread continues)

**If Personnel Become Trapped**:
- Attempt Phase-Blind rescue (if possible)
- Document personnel loss (timeline contamination)
- Administer amnestics to rescue team (prevent contamination)
- Consider personnel lost (if rescue impossible)

### SUCCESS CRITERIA

**Protocol Success**:
- Flicker event contained (reality stabilized)
- Timeline contamination neutralized (amnestic deployment successful)
- No personnel Phase Burn (medical screening clear)
- Containment maintained (no leaks)

**Protocol Failure**:
- Flicker continues (containment failed)
- Timeline contamination spreads (amnestic ineffective)
- Personnel Phase Burn (exposure occurred)
- Escalation required (Protocol JUDGMENT DAY)
