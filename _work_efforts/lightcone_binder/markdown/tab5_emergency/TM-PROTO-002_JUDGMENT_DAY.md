# TM-PROTO-002: Protocol JUDGMENT DAY

**Document Type**: Emergency Protocol  
**Classification**: TOP SECRET // ORACLE EYES ONLY  
**Tab**: 5 - Emergency Protocols

## Content Overview

Final emergency protocol for catastrophic Light Cone failure - when reality instability cannot be contained and timeline contamination threatens to spread beyond the facility. Protocol JUDGMENT DAY authorizes tactical nuclear sterilization of the affected site to prevent reality-level contamination from spreading to the general population.
