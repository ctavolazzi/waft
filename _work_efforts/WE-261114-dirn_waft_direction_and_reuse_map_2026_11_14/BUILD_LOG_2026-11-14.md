## Found while building

- **The gym did not enforce its own win conditions.** It judged answers with the InputTransformer alone, which checks shape and not the maths. Weights summing to 1.5, or negative, won "The Logic Trap". The judge now also runs the DecisionMatrixCalculator's validation (`validate_answer`, `tests/test_gym_judge.py`). FogSift's lab harness planted exactly this defect and it never registered.
- **The gym's fitness formula gives a total failure 0.54**, above its own "evolutionary death" line of 0.5, because few calls and no safety problem earn points on their own. The lab scores a failed encounter 0 and leaves the gym's formula alone.
- **The project's ruff settings check 4 files.** The exclusion list names folders such as `api`, `cli` and `docs`, and ruff applies those names anywhere in the tree. Ticket C12.
- **The Dealer writes into the working directory** on any command when he appears. That is what dirtied the checkout during tests (backlog F14). Ticket E19 asks CT what he should be allowed to do.
- **Quest files use `rewards`, the gym reads `loot_table`**, so no quest awards XP. Ticket A20.
- **Recording a passed quest checkpoint raised NameError.** The quest module lived in `scripts/`, outside every check; moving it into the package put it in front of the undefined-names pass, which found it. Fixed, with a test.
- **18 modules still do not import from a wheel** (demos with bare imports, optional tools). Ticket F24.
- **The share renderer only worked at 1080 px wide.** The GIF fallback (540 px) would have crashed. Found by a test, fixed: layouts are now in design units.
