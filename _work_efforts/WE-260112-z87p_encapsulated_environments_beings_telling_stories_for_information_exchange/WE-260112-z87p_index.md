## Objective
Create encapsulated environments where evolving Beings tell each other Stories to relay Information about How To Do Things and How To Understand What Things Are. Implement Scint as measurable Agreement between Beings, Arrow of Intent for harm tracking, and multi-layered environment simulation. Beings can safely exchange information when they Agree (Scint alignment), and must react to misalignment in stacked environments.
