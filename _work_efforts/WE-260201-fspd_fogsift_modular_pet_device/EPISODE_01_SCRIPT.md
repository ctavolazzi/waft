# Episode 1: "I'm Building a Modular Tamagotchi"

## Format
- Faceless, hands + torso only
- Bright washed-out background, soft lighting
- Soft male baritone voiceover
- Focus: build, tech, tasks, techniques

---

## Cold Open (0:00 - 0:30)

**[VISUAL]**: Hands placing two wooden devices on desk. They slide together with a satisfying magnetic click. The screens light up - a pixel fox walks from one screen to the other to meet a pixel owl.

**[VO]**: "What if your tamagotchi could meet someone else's? Not through an app. Physically. Just... click them together."

**[VISUAL]**: Pull back to show multiple devices forming a grid, a tiny pixel world spanning all screens.

---

## The Idea (0:30 - 2:00)

**[VISUAL]**: Concept renders displayed on monitor. Hands gesture at key features.

**[VO]**: "I'm building this. A modular pet device. Each one is its own little world - a creature lives inside, you take care of it, classic tamagotchi stuff."

**[VISUAL]**: Close-up of render showing magnetic edges.

**[VO]**: "But the edges have magnets. When you connect two devices, they talk to each other. The screens become one. Your creature walks over to say hello."

**[VISUAL]**: Show the various configurations - 2x2 grid, long line, tabletop integration.

**[VO]**: "Two devices, four devices, a whole wall of them. The world just... grows."

---

## Why I'm Building This (2:00 - 3:00)

**[VISUAL]**: Hands sketching rough diagrams on paper.

**[VO]**: "I grew up with tamagotchis. They were magic - this little life in your pocket. But they were also isolated. Your pet, alone, forever."

**[VISUAL]**: Two old tamagotchi devices shown, not connecting.

**[VO]**: "What if they weren't? What if the physical act of putting two things together created something new?"

**[VISUAL]**: Back to the concept renders - creatures interacting.

**[VO]**: "That's the experiment."

---

## What Needs to Happen (3:00 - 5:00)

**[VISUAL]**: Hands writing on whiteboard or notepad. List appears:

**[VO]**: "To make this real, I need:"

1. **Hardware**
   - "A small display. Low resolution is fine - pixel art looks better that way."
   - "A microcontroller. Something with Bluetooth for a companion app, enough power to run animations."
   - "Battery, charging circuit, buttons."
   - "The enclosure - I want wood. Walnut. Rounded corners. Premium feel."

2. **The Connection System**
   - "Magnets to hold them together."
   - "Pogo pins to actually communicate. When they touch, they need to know."

3. **Firmware**
   - "Creature logic - hunger, mood, sleep cycles."
   - "The linking protocol - what happens when two devices meet."
   - "Display rendering, animations, the whole game loop."

4. **The Creatures**
   - "Species, evolution paths, breeding mechanics."
   - "Art - pixel sprites, animations, environments."

**[VISUAL]**: Hands circling the whole list.

**[VO]**: "That's the map. Let's start walking."

**[VISUAL]**: Devices on desk, one screen showing "TO BE CONTINUED" in pixel font.

## Voiceover Notes

**Tone**: Calm, deliberate, slightly curious. Not hyped. Let the idea speak.

**Pacing**: Unhurried. Pauses are fine. This isn't a race.

**Avoid**:
- "Hey guys"
- "Smash that subscribe button"
- Self-deprecation
- Over-explanation

**Embrace**:
- Confidence without arrogance
- Technical specificity
- Showing, not just telling
