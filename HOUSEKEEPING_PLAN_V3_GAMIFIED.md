## 🎮 New Metric System

Instead of "hours" and "weeks", we measure work in **WAFT's native game mechanics**:

### 📊 The Four Metrics

#### 1. **Scint Cost** (✨) - Energy Required
- **Low**: 10-30 Scint (trivial task, autopilot mode)
- **Medium**: 40-70 Scint (focused effort required)
- **High**: 80-150 Scint (deep work, mentally demanding)
- **Epic**: 200+ Scint (marathon session, hero mode)

#### 2. **Karma Impact** (☯️) - Ethical/Structural Alignment
- **Chaos** (-50 to -10): Breaking things, risk, disorder
- **Neutral** (0): No moral dimension
- **Order** (+10 to +50): Creating structure, reducing entropy

#### 3. **Integrity Cost** (💚) - System Stability Risk
- **Safe**: 0-10 damage (won't break anything)
- **Careful**: 15-30 damage (minor risk, easy recovery)
- **Risky**: 40-60 damage (could break things, plan needed)
- **Dangerous**: 70-100 damage (high risk, backup essential)

#### 4. **Cognitive Load** (🧠) - Mental Complexity
- **Trivial**: 1 unit (can do while watching TV)
- **Simple**: 2-3 units (needs attention but routine)
- **Moderate**: 4-6 units (requires focus, some thinking)
- **Complex**: 7-9 units (deep thought, problem solving)
- **Intense**: 10+ units (peak mental performance needed)
