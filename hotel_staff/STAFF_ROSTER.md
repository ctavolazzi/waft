## Software Engineering Principles

Each staff member embodies specific principles:

- **Separation of Concerns**: Each role has distinct responsibilities
- **DRY (Don't Repeat Yourself)**: Staff share knowledge but don't duplicate work
- **Composable**: Staff work together seamlessly
- **Maintainable**: Each role is well-defined and maintainable
