You reach for the leather pouch. The moment your fingers touch it, a chill runs up your arm. The figure smiles—a knowing, unsettling smile.

*"Excellent choice. The crypt entrance is behind the mill's waterwheel. Speak the word 'Vexaris' and the stones will part."*

They press a tarnished silver amulet into your palm. *"Wear this. It will... protect you. Somewhat."*

The amulet is cold to the touch, and you could swear you hear faint whispering coming from it.

*"Return before dawn,"* the figure adds, *"or don't return at all."*

As you leave the tavern, the night air hits you like a wall. The old mill looms on the hill, its broken sails silhouetted against the moon. A faint green light pulses from within.

How do you proceed?
