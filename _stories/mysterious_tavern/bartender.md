You settle onto a worn barstool. The half-orc bartender approaches, polishing a glass with a rag that's seen better days.

*"What'll it be, traveler?"* His voice is surprisingly gentle for someone his size.

Behind him, shelves groan under the weight of exotic bottles—some glowing, some bubbling, one that appears to contain a tiny storm. A sign reads: **"House Special: 3 gold. Effects may vary."**

The bartender follows your gaze and grins, revealing a gold tooth.

*"First time at the Serpent? Name's Grok. Been pouring drinks here for twenty years. Seen a lot of strange folk come through..."* He glances meaningfully toward the hooded figure in the corner.

What would you like to discuss?
