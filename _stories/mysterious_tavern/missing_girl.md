You ask Grok about the missing girl. His expression softens.

*"Lily Fairweather. Sweet kid. Used to come in here with her father sometimes—he'd have ale, she'd have milk and cookies."*

He sighs heavily. *"She vanished a week ago. Was playing near the old mill when it happened. Her father's been half-mad with grief. Posted that reward himself, though I doubt the town council approves."*

*"Any idea what happened to her?"*

Grok hesitates. *"Folk say... folk say she was drawn there. Like something called to her. The night she disappeared, witnesses saw green light at the mill and heard her laughing."*

He shakes his head. *"Laughing. Like she'd found something wonderful."*

You could approach Lord Fairweather for more information, or go straight to the mill where she disappeared.

What do you choose?
