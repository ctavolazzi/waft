---
title: The House Special
---

*"One house special coming up!"* Grok grins and turns to the mysterious bottles.

He selects three: one glowing amber, one swirling purple, one that seems to contain liquid starlight. With practiced movements, he combines them into a tall glass. The result is a drink that shifts colors constantly, tiny lights floating within like captured fireflies.

*"Bottoms up!"* He winks.

You drink. The taste is indescribable—sweet, then bitter, then something that might be the concept of *possibility* given liquid form.

The world spins. Colors become sounds. Sounds become colors. You see, for one brilliant moment, the threads of fate connecting everyone in the tavern. And you understand: the hooded figure, the mill, the missing people—it's all connected. A web of destiny you were never meant to see.

Then it fades. You're back on your barstool, feeling lighter, wiser, and slightly purple.

---

*The next morning, you remembered nothing of your vision. But something had changed. When you eventually left town, you found yourself walking a different path—one that led to adventures beyond imagining. The Gilded Serpent became a fond memory, and Grok's house special became a story you'd tell for years.*

**Sometimes the journey begins with a single drink.**
