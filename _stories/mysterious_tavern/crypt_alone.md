---
title: The Crypt Awaits
scene: crypt
mood: danger
---

You speak the word *"Vexaris"* and watch as ancient stones grind apart, revealing a stairway descending into absolute darkness.

The amulet around your neck grows warm. The whispering intensifies—you can almost make out words now. *"...turn back... doom... treasure... glory..."*

Taking a deep breath, you descend.

The crypt is vast, far larger than should fit beneath an old mill. Phosphorescent fungi provide dim light, revealing rows of stone sarcophagi stretching into the distance. And there, at the center of the chamber, sits a pedestal bearing a single object:

A **heart**. Still beating. Glowing with inner fire.

As you approach, the sarcophagi begin to *open*.

You grab the heart and run.

---

*You emerged from the crypt as dawn broke, clutching the impossible heart. The hooded figure was gone from the tavern—but on your table sat three vials and a note: "Well done. We'll meet again."*

*The heart still beats in your pack. Sometimes, late at night, you swear it whispers your name.*

**Your adventure continues...**
