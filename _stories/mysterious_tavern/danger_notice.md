You study the danger notice more closely. The town seal is authentic—this is an official warning from the local authority.

Reading between the lines, you gather:
- Strange lights have been seen at the old mill for two weeks
- Three people have gone missing after investigating
- The town guard has been *forbidden* from approaching (interesting...)
- A 100 gold bounty exists for "information leading to resolution"

Someone has scrawled in the margin: *"The guards are scared. Whatever's there, it's not natural."*

The mill sits on a hill at the edge of town. You can see it from here through the tavern window—a dark silhouette against the moonlit sky. As you watch, a faint green light pulses from within.

Something is definitely happening there. The question is: what do you do about it?
