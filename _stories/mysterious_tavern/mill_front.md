---
title: Through the Front Door
scene: mill_interior
mood: supernatural
---

Stealth be damned—you push through the front door. The hinges scream in protest, announcing your presence to anything within.

The mill's interior is exactly what you'd expect: rotting grain sacks, rusted machinery, the skeleton of what was once a thriving business. But at the center of the room stands something impossible:

A **portal**. A shimmering tear in reality, edges crackling with green energy. Through it, you glimpse another world—purple skies, floating stones, creatures that defy description.

And standing before the portal, arms raised in concentration, is a young girl. The same gap-toothed smile from the lost notice, but her eyes now glow with alien light.

She turns to you. *"You shouldn't have come. But since you're here... would you like to see what's on the other side?"*

The portal pulses invitingly.

---

*You stepped through the portal. What you found on the other side was beyond human comprehension—beauty and horror intertwined. When you returned (hours later? years?), the mill was empty. The girl was gone. But you carried something back with you. Something that would change everything.*

**Some doors, once opened, can never be closed.**
