You weave through the crowded tables toward the hooded figure. As you approach, they look up—revealing a face marked by age and arcane scars. Their eyes shimmer with an unnatural silver light.

*"I've been waiting for someone like you,"* they whisper. *"Someone who isn't afraid of shadows."*

They slide a small leather pouch across the table. It jingles with what sounds like coins—but there's something else inside. Something that *moves*.

*"There's a crypt beneath the old mill,"* they continue. *"Something ancient has awakened. Bring me what you find there, and I'll make you wealthy beyond imagination."*

The glowing blue drink pulses once, as if punctuating their offer.

What do you do?
