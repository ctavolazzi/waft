---
title: The Town Guard
---

You find the guard station mostly empty—just one tired deputy manning the desk, a half-eaten sandwich his only company.

*"The mill?"* He goes pale. *"Look, friend, I'll be straight with you. Captain ordered us not to go near that place. Three of our best went in last week. Only one came back, and he..."*

The deputy shudders. *"He won't stop screaming. Even sedated, even asleep—he screams about eyes in the dark and something that's *waiting*."*

He leans forward. *"You want my advice? Whatever's in that mill, leave it alone. Let it stay buried. Some things are better left—"*

A green flash illuminates the window. You both turn to see the mill on the hill, now glowing brighter than ever.

*"Gods preserve us,"* the deputy whispers. *"It's starting again."*

---

*That night, the mill burned. No one knows who set the fire—some say it was you, others say the fire came from within. When dawn broke, only ashes remained. The disappearances stopped. The strange lights were never seen again. But sometimes, late at night, old-timers swear they can still see a faint green glow on the hill where the mill once stood.*

**Some mysteries are best left unsolved.**
