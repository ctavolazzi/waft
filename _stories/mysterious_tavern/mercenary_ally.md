---
title: Hired Steel
---

The mercenaries listen to your offer with professional interest. Their leader, a scarred woman named Vera, names her price: *"Fifty gold upfront, plus a third of whatever we find."*

It's steep, but their reputation precedes them. The **Iron Ravens** have never lost a client.

The crypt is everything the stories warned of—and worse. But the Ravens earn their gold. When skeletal guardians rise from their tombs, Vera's crew cuts them down with practiced efficiency. When poisoned darts fly from the walls, their shield bearer deflects them all.

You reach the beating heart together. Vera eyes it greedily but keeps her word. *"A deal's a deal. That's yours."*

---

*The Iron Ravens became your trusted companions. With the heart's power and their steel, you carved out a small kingdom of your own. Vera became your general. The hooded figure never appeared again—but sometimes, in dreams, you see those silver eyes watching.*

**Power attracts power.**
