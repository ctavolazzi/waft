You slip out of the tavern and make your way up the hill. The old mill looms larger as you approach, its broken sails creaking in the night wind. The green light you saw earlier pulses steadily from a gap in the boarded windows.

Up close, the mill is more ruin than building. Ivy covers the stone walls. The waterwheel is frozen in place, choked with weeds and debris. But someone—or something—has been here recently. Fresh footprints in the mud. Scratches on the door frame.

Two options present themselves:

The **front door** hangs off its hinges, barely attached. Through it, you can see a faint glow—probably the source of those strange lights.

Around the back, you notice a **cellar entrance** half-hidden by overgrown shrubs. Darker, but potentially safer.

How do you proceed?
