Grok's friendly expression darkens. He leans close, voice dropping to a whisper.

*"That one's been here three nights now. Orders the same drink—something I ain't never seen before. Brews it themselves."*

He polishes a glass nervously. *"Folk who talk to them... some come back rich. Others don't come back at all. Last week, a merchant sat with them for an hour. Next morning, they found him at the old mill, aged fifty years overnight."*

The bartender shakes his head. *"I don't judge my customers. But if I were you? I'd find another table to sit at tonight."*

He slides you a drink on the house. *"For your health."*
