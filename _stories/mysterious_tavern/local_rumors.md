Grok chuckles. *"Rumors? Friend, this town runs on 'em."*

He counts on thick fingers: *"First, there's the old mill. Three people gone missing there this month. Town council's scared stiff but won't do nothing about it."*

*"Second, Lord Fairweather's daughter vanished a week back. He's offering a fortune to anyone who finds her, but search parties keep coming back empty-handed. Or not coming back at all."*

*"Third..."* He glances around and lowers his voice. *"Some folk say they've seen lights in the sky at night. Moving lights. And shapes that don't belong to any bird I know."*

He straightens up. *"But hey, probably just too much ale talking, right?"*
