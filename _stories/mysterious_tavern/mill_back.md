---
title: The Back Entrance
---

You creep around to the cellar entrance, carefully moving aside the overgrown shrubs. The wooden doors are old but solid—and locked from the inside.

A soft voice drifts up from below: *"I know you're there. I can smell the fear on you."*

Your blood runs cold. The voice is sweet, childlike—but wrong somehow.

*"Don't worry. I won't hurt you. I just want someone to talk to. It's so lonely down here."*

You hear the lock click open from within. The cellar doors swing outward on their own.

Stone steps descend into darkness. The same green light you saw earlier pulses from below, illuminating a narrow corridor carved from bedrock.

*"Come down,"* the voice whispers. *"I have so much to show you."*

---

*You descended into the depths. What you found there was Lord Fairweather's missing daughter—but changed, transformed by forces beyond this world. She had become something between human and god. You had a choice: help her complete her transformation, or try to bring her back. The fate of the entire region hung in the balance.*

*This is where your true adventure began.*

**The most dangerous places often house the most important choices.**
