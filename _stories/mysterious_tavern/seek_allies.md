Wisdom stays your feet. A crypt full of unknown dangers is no place to go alone.

You scan the tavern for potential allies. Two options stand out:

**The Bard**: A young half-elf with nimble fingers and knowing eyes. Their songs speak of ancient places and forgotten treasures—perhaps they know something useful. Plus, bards are said to have a touch of magic.

**The Mercenaries**: Three hardened warriors sit at a corner table, their scarred armor telling tales of countless battles. They look expensive—but effective.

Time is short. Dawn approaches. You need to choose quickly.

Who do you approach?
