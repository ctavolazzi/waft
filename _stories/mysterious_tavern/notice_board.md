You approach the notice board near the entrance. Layers of parchment overlap each other, some yellowed with age, others fresh with still-wet ink.

Three notices catch your eye:

**1. DANGER** (written in red ink, with official town seal)
*"Citizens warned: Do NOT approach the old mill after dark. Strange lights reported. Three missing persons this month."*

**2. HELP WANTED** (simple handwriting)
*"Seeking brave adventurer for escort mission to Silverdale. Good pay. Ask for Mira at the Inn."*

**3. LOST** (tear-stained parchment)
*"My daughter went missing near the old mill. 500 gold reward. No questions asked. -Desperate Father"*

The lost notice has a small portrait attached—a young girl with bright eyes and a gap-toothed smile.

Which notice draws your attention?
