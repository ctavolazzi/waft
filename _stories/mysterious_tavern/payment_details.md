You lean back, crossing your arms. *"Wealthy beyond imagination is rather vague. What exactly are we talking about?"*

The hooded figure chuckles—a dry, rasping sound. *"A shrewd one. Good. You'll need that."*

They open their cloak slightly, revealing a belt lined with vials of glowing liquid. *"These are distilled memories of heroes long dead. Each one grants abilities beyond mortal ken—for a time."*

They tap one vial. *"This one belonged to a legendary swordmaster. Another contains the cunning of a master thief."*

*"Bring me what lies in the crypt, and I'll give you three vials of your choosing. Plus..."* They produce a small bag of gold. *"...five hundred gold upfront. A gesture of good faith."*

The offer has improved considerably. But the way the vials *pulse* in rhythm with each other is deeply unsettling.

What's your decision?
