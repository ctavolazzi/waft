---
title: A Simple Job
---

You find Mira at a corner table—a weathered merchant woman with kind eyes and calloused hands.

*"Thank the gods someone answered,"* she sighs with relief. *"I need to get medical supplies to Silverdale. The mountain pass is usually safe, but lately..."* She trails off.

*"Lately what?"*

*"Bandits. Or something worse. Three caravans have disappeared this month. But my sister is sick. These medicines might save her life."*

She offers twenty gold for the journey, plus room and board in Silverdale. It's honest work, far from the strange happenings at the mill.

You accept. Sometimes the best adventures are the simple ones—helping those in need, seeing new places, making honest coin.

---

*The journey to Silverdale was harder than expected. There were bandits—and something in the mountains that made them look friendly by comparison. But you delivered the medicine. Mira's sister recovered. And you gained a reputation as someone reliable, someone who finishes what they start. The mysterious tavern faded to memory, but the skills you learned on that mountain road stayed with you forever.*

**The greatest heroes are those who help others without seeking glory.**
