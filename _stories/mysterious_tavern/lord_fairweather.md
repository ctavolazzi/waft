---
title: A Father's Grief
---

Lord Fairweather's manor sits at the town's edge—a grand house that's seen better days. The gardens are overgrown, the windows dark. Only a single light burns in an upper room.

A servant answers your knock, eyes red from crying. *"The lord... he's not seeing visitors."*

But you're persistent, and eventually you're led to a study. Lord Fairweather sits alone, a portrait of his daughter clutched to his chest. He's aged a decade in a week.

*"You... you're here about Lily?"* His voice cracks. *"Please. Tell me you can bring her back."*

He tells you everything: how Lily had always been *special*, able to see things others couldn't. How she'd started talking about *friends* at the mill. How he'd forbidden her from going there—until the night she vanished.

*"I'll give you everything I have,"* he whispers. *"Just bring my daughter home."*

---

*You promised him you'd try. Armed with his information and his blessing, you set out for the mill. What you found there would change your understanding of reality itself. Lord Fairweather got his daughter back eventually—but she was never quite the same. Neither were you.*

**Love drives us to face horrors beyond imagination.**
