---
title: The Wise Choice
---

Something about this feels wrong. The pulsing vials, the silver eyes, the impossible offer. Every instinct screams *trap*.

*"I appreciate the offer,"* you say, standing, *"but I'll pass."*

The hooded figure's expression doesn't change, but you sense disappointment—or perhaps respect. *"A shame. The crypt will claim another seeker tonight. Perhaps they'll be less... cautious."*

You return to the bar, order a simple ale, and find a quiet corner. Throughout the night, you notice others approach the hooded figure. Some leave excited. Others leave pale.

By morning, the figure is gone. A week later, you hear rumors: three adventurers found dead at the old mill, their bodies drained of all color.

---

*You stayed in the town for a few more days, then moved on. Sometimes you wonder what was in that crypt. But you're still alive to wonder—and that's worth more than any treasure.*

**Not every adventure needs to be accepted. Wisdom is knowing which quests to refuse.**
