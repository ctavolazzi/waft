You push open the heavy oak door of **The Gilded Serpent** tavern. The warmth hits you first, then the smell of roasted meat and spilled ale. A fire crackles in the hearth, casting dancing shadows across the worn wooden floor.

The common room is lively tonight. Merchants argue over trade routes in one corner. A bard strums a melancholy tune near the fire. And in the darkest corner sits a *hooded figure*, nursing a drink that glows faintly blue.

The bartender—a burly half-orc with surprisingly kind eyes—nods in your direction. Behind him, you notice a board covered in yellowed parchments and official-looking notices.

What draws your attention?
