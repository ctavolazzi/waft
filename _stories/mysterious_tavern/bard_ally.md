---
title: The Bard's Knowledge
---

The bard's eyes light up when you mention the crypt. *"The Tomb of Vexaris? By the gods, I thought it was just a legend!"*

Their name is Lyric, and they've spent years collecting stories about the old mill. *"There's supposed to be a heart down there—the heart of an ancient king who made a deal with something beyond the stars. They say whoever possesses it can command the dead."*

Together, you venture into the crypt. Lyric's songs keep the shadows at bay, and their knowledge of ancient traps saves your life twice.

When you find the beating heart, Lyric hesitates. *"This... this shouldn't exist. If we take it, we're changing the world forever."*

But you came this far. You take the heart.

---

*You and Lyric became legends—the ones who raided the Tomb of Vexaris and lived. The heart now sits in a secure vault, studied by the greatest minds of the realm. Sometimes Lyric writes songs about that night. They never quite capture how terrifying it really was.*

**The best adventures are shared.**
