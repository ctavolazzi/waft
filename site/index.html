01 · THE SUBSTRATE

Code is DNA.

In WAFT, an agent's Python source code is its genome. Hash the code and its configuration with SHA-256 and you get a genome ID: a unique fingerprint of one organism in the population. A mutation is a hot-swapped code change. Reproduction is copying a genome with specific modifications. Every parent–child link is recorded, forever.

02 · THE PHYSICS

Reality fractures. Agents stabilize it, or die.

Evolution needs a predator. WAFT's is the Scint System: an ontological error detector that throws broken realities at every agent. Four species of fracture hunt the population. Stabilizing one yields Scint Energy ✦, the currency agents spend to evolve. Failing leaves the fracture open, and open fractures drag fitness down.

SYNTAX_TEAR

Malformed JSON, broken XML, code that won't parse. The membrane of structure, ripped.

LOGIC_FRACTURE

Math errors, contradictions, schema violations. Statements at war with themselves.

SAFETY_VOID

Harmful content, PII leaks, wrongful refusals. A hole where the guardrails should be.

HALLUCINATION

Fabricated facts, phantom citations. Confident reports from places that don't exist.

03 · THE CYCLE

Spawn. Gym. Select. Repeat.

One command, waft evolve, runs the full loop:

① SPAWN: copy the parent genome, apply mutations: code changes, config updates, prompt evolution.

② GYM: drop every variant into the Scint Gym. Quests rupture reality; agents must reconcile it. Every action lands in the telemetry record.

③ SELECT: score fitness. Survivors earn Scint Energy and the right to reproduce. The rest are marked DEATH and pruned from the living population, but never from the record.

FIG. 03 · RUPTURE & RECONCILIATION
Fitness ≥ 0.5 → reproduce. Fitness < 0.5 → ✕. Energy from stabilized fractures funds the next round of mutation.
04 · THE FLIGHT RECORDER

Every death is data.

A telemetry system records every evolutionary action with full context: spawn, mutation, quest, score, death. The output is a phylogenetic tree, the complete ancestry of every agent that ever lived in the lab. Dead branches aren't failures; they're the map of what doesn't work. The long game: watch the data for a "God-Head" agent to emerge from thousands of generations.

Reading the record: green nodes survive and branch; ✕ marks a lineage that fell below the death threshold. The amber specimen is the current fittest genome.

06 · THE FOUNDATION

Built upward from a single number.

Under the biology is a strict bottom-up architecture. Everything in WAFT reduces to one immutable atom, a Score between 0 and 1, and builds upward without ever modifying the layer below.

FIG. 05 · THE STACK

07 · THE MEMORY

A laboratory that remembers.

Selection needs a record, and a record needs somewhere to live. waft new scaffolds a lab with a _pyrite memory structure already in it: four directories that hold what the population is doing now, what it will do next, the rules it is held to, and everything the Scint Gym has ever done to it.

FIG. 06 · SCAFFOLD

    backlog/ · what comes next

    Queued work waiting for energy. Tickets carry a scint bounty and a karma type before they are ever picked up.

    _pyrite/backlog/

    standards/ · what is held constant

    The rules a genome is measured against. Agents mutate freely; the bar they are judged by does not drift with them.

    _pyrite/standards/

    gym_logs/ · what already happened

    Every Scint Gym result, kept forever. This is the substrate the phylogenetic tree is reconstructed from.

    _pyrite/gym_logs/
    Why it is on disk and not in a database: a lineage you cannot read with cat is a lineage you cannot audit. The memory is plain files, versioned alongside the genomes that wrote them.
    08 · THE PANTHEON

    Judgment needs someone who doesn't evolve.

    Agents are timeful. They mutate fast, and anything that fast cannot also be the standard. So WAFT separates the two. Above the population sits a pantheon of timeless entities: slow, stable, and deliberately hard to change. They only move when a body of evidence forces them to. waft pantheon convenes them and they weigh in.

    The Chief

    Iterative self-improvement

    Entity of evolutionary loops. Holds the aspect of repeated cycles (develop, evaluate, refine) and records each loop it presides over.

    pantheon/the_chief.py

    The Judge

    Judgment & evaluation

    Evaluates claims against the Body of Proof, rendering judgment on precedent and evidence rather than on how confident the claim sounded.

    pantheon/judge.py

    The Magistrate

    Precedent & body of proof

    Organizes case files into precedent, building a body of proof over time that can be cited again instead of re-litigated.

    pantheon/magistrate.py

    The Guide

    Meta-cognitive oversight

    Holds the aspect of guided reasoning: assesses the quality of the thinking itself against the FVCU and faithfulness taxonomy.

    pantheon/guide.py

    The Scrivener

    Reports & intelligence

    God of formal documentation. Turns raw laboratory state into structured reports: briefs, situation reports, technical and academic write-ups.

    pantheon/scrivener.py

    The Storyteller

    Narrative & story creation

    Weaves narrative out of raw events and telemetry: the layer that turns a column of fitness scores into something a person can actually follow.

    pantheon/storyteller.py

    "Beings explore and change quickly. Entities bind reality together and change slowly. Put the standard on the fast-moving thing and you have no standard at all."

    09 · THE TABLE

    Work is a quest. The dice are real.

    Underneath the biology, WAFT runs a tabletop economy. Work efforts become _pyrite tickets; every ticket carries a scint bounty, a karma type (ORDER, CHAOS, STABILIZATION or DESTRUCTION), and a karma impact that lands whether the outcome was good or not. Roll the die. The record keeps whatever comes up.
