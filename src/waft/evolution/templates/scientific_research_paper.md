# Scientific Research Paper Template for WAFT Self-Study

**Purpose**: Comprehensive template for WAFT to conduct scientific research on itself using the scientific method.

**Integration**: Works with WAFT's evolutionary document creator, Study Gym, and lineage tracking systems.

- **Flight Recorder**: Track study lineage
- **Component Evolution**: Evolve paper structure over time
- **User Feedback**: Learn from paper quality feedback
