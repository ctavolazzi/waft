---
title: The Mushroom Grove
---

You kneel down to examine the glowing mushrooms more closely. As your hand approaches, they suddenly **release a cloud of sparkling spores** that fill the air around you.

You breathe in deeply, and the world begins to shimmer and shift. Visions flood your mind:

- Ancient miners working these tunnels
- A great underground city, now abandoned
- A sleeping dragon in a chamber of gold

The visions fade, and you find yourself back at the cave entrance with no memory of how you got there. But you carry with you the **knowledge of what lies beneath**.
