---
title: The Depths Below
---

You take a deep breath and dive into the underground river. The current is stronger than you expected, and it pulls you downward into a massive underwater chamber.

Your lungs burning, you're about to panic when you realize: **you can breathe underwater here**. The bioluminescent algae has released oxygen into the water, creating a breathable liquid environment.

You explore for hours, discovering the ruins of an ancient aquatic civilization. Their murals tell of a people who chose to live beneath the waves rather than above them.

Eventually, you find an exit that leads to a hidden lagoon outside the mountain. You emerge transformed, carrying the secret of the underwater realm.
