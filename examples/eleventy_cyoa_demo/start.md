You stand at the entrance to a mysterious cavern. The air is cool and damp, and you can hear the faint sound of water echoing from deep within. Strange symbols are carved into the stone archway above the entrance.

Two paths lie before you: one leads into complete darkness, the other slopes downward toward the sound of flowing water.
