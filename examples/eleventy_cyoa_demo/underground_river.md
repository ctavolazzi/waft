You follow the sloping passage downward until you reach a magnificent **underground river**. The water is crystal clear and flows swiftly through a natural channel carved into the bedrock over thousands of years.

Bioluminescent algae line the river's edge, casting an eerie green glow across the cavern walls. You can see the river continues both upstream and downstream into darkness.
