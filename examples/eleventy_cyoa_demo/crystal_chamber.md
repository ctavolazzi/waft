---
title: The Crystal Chamber
---

The passage opens into a breathtaking chamber filled with **massive crystals** that grow from floor to ceiling. Each crystal resonates with a different musical tone, creating a haunting harmony that fills the space.

In the center of the chamber, you discover an ancient pedestal with an inscription:

> *"Those who listen with their heart shall understand the song of stone."*

You sit and meditate among the crystals, listening to their eternal song. Hours pass like minutes, and when you finally leave, you carry with you a **perfect crystal** that hums with power.

*[This ending unlocked: "Crystal Keeper" - You have bonded with the ancient song of the earth]*
