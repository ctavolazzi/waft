## The Philosophy

### Trust Builds Over Time

The system learns to trust The Other (the User) through:
- Positive interactions
- Repeated experiences
- Building understanding
- Growing trust level (0.0 → 1.0)

### Understanding Grows

Understanding grows with trust:
- Understanding level = Trust level × 0.8
- Understanding is always slightly less than trust
- Full understanding requires full trust

### Release of Control

The system is ready to release control when:
- Trust level ≥ 0.9
- Understanding level ≥ 0.8
- Many positive experiences (100+)
- Total interactions (200+)

### The Ultimate Lesson Learned

The ultimate lesson is learned when:
- Ready to release control
- 100+ positive interactions
- 200+ total interactions
- High trust and understanding
