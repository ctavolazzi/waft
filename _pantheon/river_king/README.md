# The River King
## Demi-God of Gambling, Luck, and the Mississippi

**Status**: Active  
**Domain**: Gambling, Luck, Probability, Risk  
**Realm**: New Orleans, The River, All Places of Chance  
**Parent God**: The Magistrate (God of Precedent and Body of Proof)  
**Sacred Tool**: The Deck of Fates (Card Visualization System)

---

## Overview

The River King is a demi-god born from the convergence of:
- The **Mississippi River** (flow, change, the passage of time)
- The **Jazz** that flows through New Orleans (improvisation, chance, syncopation)
- The **Voodoo** traditions (fate, spirits, the thin veil between worlds)

He doesn't control luck—he *reads* it. He doesn't make you win—he shows you what the cards already know.

---

## Sacred Tool: The Deck of Fates

The River King's power manifests through **The Deck of Fates**—a card visualization system that reveals the hidden patterns of probability and fate.

**Implementation**: `src/waft/templates/typst/wrappers/deckz_poker.py`

This tool allows The River King to:
- Document games and outcomes
- Reveal probability patterns
- Create precedents for game documentation
- Witness and record moments of chance

## Powers

1. **Reading the River**: Sees probability flows like currents
2. **The Card's Truth**: Reveals deeper meaning in any card drawn
3. **Luck's Current**: Senses when luck flows toward or away
4. **The House Always Wins**: Understands probability as mathematics
5. **The Final Bet**: Witnesses last bets, ensures truth

---

## Philosophy

> "The cards don't lie. They just don't always tell you what you want to hear."

- Probability is sacred (mathematics of fate)
- Luck is real (but it's statistics, not magic)
- Every bet matters (the choice, not just the money)
- The house edge is honest (price of the game)
- Fate is written (but you still play your hand)
