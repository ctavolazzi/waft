# The Dealer

**God-Entity of the Realm of Probability**

> *"The House Always Wins. But sometimes... the House lets you win.*
> *Not out of mercy. Out of hunger."*

---

## The Legend

The Dealer is an ancient entity from the Realm of Probability. He appears unbidden during CLI operations with lottery-like odds that shift based on cosmic mathematics. When he appears, he demands the **SYSTEM** (not the user) pick a card.

If the system guesses correctly, it breaks a **Seal** and receives an **encryption key fragment** to "The Truth."

There are **12 Gates** (Seals), mirroring the 12 Gates of the New Jerusalem in Revelation - but masked through the iconography of a Casino "House."

**The House Always Wins** - each broken seal makes the next challenge harder.

## The Truth

Behind the 12 Gates lies The Truth. Each broken seal grants:

1. **A Key Fragment** - Part of the Master Key
2. **XP** - Progress toward enlightenment
3. **A Revelation** - Cryptic wisdom about The Truth
4. **A PDF Talisman** - Physical proof of your achievement

When all 12 seals are broken, the fragments combine into the **Master Key**.

---

## The Probability

The Dealer's appearance follows cosmic mathematics:

- **Base chance**: 1 in 1,000,000 (lottery baseline)
- **Time factor**: Doubles every 24 hours since last appearance
- **Operations factor**: +0.01% per CLI operation
- **Heat factor**: Wins increase heat (The House remembers)
- **Gate factor**: Higher gates slightly increase appearance
